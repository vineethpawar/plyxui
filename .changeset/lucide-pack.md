lucidePack: 50 production icons generated from lucide geometry (ISC), replacing the need for hand-drawn seeds. Same registry, same cross-platform renderers — register after seedPack and overlapping names upgrade to lucide weights. Brand icons (github, linkedin) ship with classic feather-lineage paths since lucide removed them upstream. Regenerate any time with packages/icons/scripts/gen-lucide-pack.mjs.
